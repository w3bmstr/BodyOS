The Science
Core mechanisms — why this protocol works
Master switch
mTOR — growth activator, autophagy suppressor
Activated by food, insulin, and sugar. When mTOR is ON, autophagy is OFF and cellular growth is ON. Turned OFF by fasting, exercise, green tea EGCG, caloric restriction, and some compounds like berberine and metformin. This is the primary switch you're toggling every time you complete the fast window.
Energy sensor
AMPK — the autophagy activator
Activated when cellular energy (ATP) is low — during fasting and exercise. AMPK directly phosphorylates and activates ULK1, the initiating kinase of autophagy. Also activated by ginger compounds (6-gingerol), EGCG, berberine, and resveratrol at food-achievable doses for autophagy support.
Cellular cleanup
Autophagy — digesting the damaged
Cells form double-membrane vesicles (autophagosomes) that engulf damaged proteins, dysfunctional organelles, and misfolded components, then fuse with lysosomes for digestion and recycling. Works at the level of individual cells — cannot clear established multicellular tumor colonies which have already disabled these pathways.
First responders
NK cells — natural killer immune surveillance
Innate immune cells that continuously patrol for abnormal cells — virally infected, pre-cancerous, and cancerous. Kill on contact via perforin and granzyme without prior sensitisation. Activity is suppressed by cortisol, alcohol, poor sleep, and smoking. Activated by garlic allicin, exercise, vitamin D, and adequate sleep.
Adaptive killers
T cells — the targeted immune response
CD8+ cytotoxic T cells kill cancer cells via perforin/granzyme and Fas–FasL pathways. Require three signals: antigen recognition (TCR + MHC I), co-stimulation (CD28), and cytokines (IL-2, IL-12). Suppressed by PD-L1 which tumors express. Require zinc, omega-3, and vitamin D to develop and function optimally.
The kill switch
Apoptosis — programmed cell death
Triggered by DNA damage beyond repair, targeted therapy, and T-cell attack. Cancer cells disable their apoptosis pathways — overexpressing Bcl-2 and underexpressing p53 — which is why established tumor colonies cannot be cleared by the natural compounds and food interventions that work at the individual cell level.
Prevention vs treatment: Food, fasting, and supplements work at the level of individual cells — preventing damage and clearing pre-cancerous changes before they consolidate. They cannot clear established tumor colonies, which have already rewritten their own rules. That distinction is load-bearing.
